Ordering FAQs

Find out how quotes, cleaning and delivery work.

How do I place an order? +

Add your seafood to the cart, enter your city and continue to WhatsApp. Send the prepared message and we will confirm your order details.

How do I find today’s prices? +

Rates depend on the catch, grade, preparation and delivery. Send your selection on WhatsApp for a current quote.

Can I order outside Karachi? +

Send your city and required date so we can confirm available routes, packing, timing and charges.

Which payment methods are available? +

Ask about advance payment and cash on delivery. COD availability depends on your city and order.

Can I choose how my fish is cut? +

Yes. Request a cut from the catalogue and we’ll confirm what is available for the fish and size you select. Ask how the quote is calculated before confirming.

What if I have a problem with my order? +

Message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery with your order details so we can help.

Our Story

Bringing Pakistan's Coast to Your Doorstep

Hamza Ocean Seafood was founded on a simple belief: that Pakistan's coastal waters produce seafood worthy of an international luxury brand, and that every household deserves access to it — without compromise, and without leaving home.

We work directly with trusted harbors and fishing communities along the coast, cutting out unnecessary middlemen so that what reaches your kitchen is as close to the source as possible. Every item is hand-selected for freshness, cleaned and cut to your preference, and sealed within a cold chain built to protect quality from harbor to home.

Our mission is straightforward: make premium seafood shopping effortless, trustworthy, and genuinely enjoyable — from your first WhatsApp message to the moment your order arrives. Our vision extends further still, toward a future where Hamza Ocean Seafood is the name every Pakistani household trusts first for seafood, wherever they are in the country.

As we grow, we're investing in wider coverage, faster cold-chain logistics, and an even more refined catalogue — always guided by the same principle we started with: freshness and trust, delivered with distinction.
